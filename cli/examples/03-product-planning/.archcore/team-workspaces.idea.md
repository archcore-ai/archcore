## Idea

Let users group projects under a shared "workspace" with its own members and
settings, instead of everything living under a single personal account.

## Why now

Teams keep asking for shared access; today they share one login, which breaks
attribution and security. Detailed in @.archcore/team-workspaces.prd.md.
